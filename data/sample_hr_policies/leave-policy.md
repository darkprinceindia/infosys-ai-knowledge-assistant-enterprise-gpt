# Employee leave policy
Owner: HR Operations
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Classification: HR

## Annual leave
Eligible full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year. Requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least five working days before the planned absence, except in emergencies. The reporting manager approves requests according to staffing needs.

## Sick leave
Employees should notify their manager as soon as practical. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than three consecutive working days. HR Operations resolves eligibility questions and exceptions.

## Policy precedence
The signed employment agreement and current regional policy take precedence if they differ from this sample policy. Employees should confirm consequential cases with HR Operations.
