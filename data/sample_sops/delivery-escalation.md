# Delivery escalation SOP
Owner: Delivery Operations
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Classification: Internal

## Severity assessment
Classify a delivery incident as P1 when a client-facing production service is unavailable or a critical contractual milestone is at immediate risk. P2 covers significant degradation with a workaround. Record the affected service, impact, start time, and incident owner.

## Escalation steps
For P1 incidents, notify the on-call delivery manager within 15 minutes, open an incident record, and start a bridge with the engineering lead. Notify the account lead within 30 minutes. The delivery manager owns client updates every 30 minutes until recovery.

## Closure
The incident owner documents the timeline, customer impact, resolution, and follow-up actions. A post-incident review is due within two working days for P1 incidents.
